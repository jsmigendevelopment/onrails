# Decision 433-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Decision:** `check-issue-size.mjs` closes the four follow-ups of round 3 on #429. (1) "so", "and", "then" and "but" end a clause only when the split comes right after them, with at most a few filler words between ("we", "it was", "already", "now", "also"). "not so easy to split into parts" and "too risky and costly to split into parts" pass again, and "too big and split into ...", "too big so we split it ...", "too big but already split" still fail. A clause is now read from the whole reason, so the lookahead sees the split. The phrase "split into" or "splitting into" followed by "would", "will", "could" or "might" within two words is a consequence, not a split done ("mechanical rename: splitting into parts would break the build" passes); "child issues" and "children 12 and 13" in the same reason still fail. (2) A number right after "tests" that is followed by a letter ("tests - 2fa flow", "test files — 3d viewer") isn't a size. A count of files isn't one either: one number under 10 after "test files", or one number under 10 before "files" or "new files" ("test files: 2", "tests – 2 new files"). Every other shape keeps its result: "tests, 600", "tests; 600", "tests/600", "tests: about ~600", "tests add ~600" and "test lines: 600" still pass, and "tests: 1k", "tests: 600 lines", "tests - 600", "test files: 600" and "tests – 600" still fail. (3) A size next to a read Size line is also found when a word or a bracket sits between "size" and the separator ("Size estimate: 1,500 lines", "Size (est.): 1,500 lines") or the separator is an arrow ("Size → 1,500 lines"). All three go in `HIDDEN` in the guard test, with three design notes that must pass in `NOTES`. (4) `consistency.md` item 12 names the exit 2 for a split parent with no `labels`. (#433)
- **Options considered:** for (1), the review's regex (misses "too big but already split", which fails on main), a longer filler list (chosen), or dropping the joiners (brings back "too big and split into child issues"); for the "would break" reason, accept any split phrase followed by a modal (also lets "splitting into child issues would be cleaner" through, which fails on main) or only the "split into" phrase, not "child issues" (chosen). For (2), the review's fix alone (leaves "test files: 2" and "tests – 2 new files" refused), or a rule that any number below 10 after "tests" is a count (also frees "tests: 5", a shape the issue doesn't name), or the narrow file-count rule (chosen). For (3), read any text between "size" and the separator (refuses design notes like "Size of the page: 12px"), or only "estimate", "est." and one bracket (chosen).
- **Why:** The check guards against honest mistakes. Each change was meant to name only the shapes the issue lists, but the review of #523 found more that moved: a differential probe over 97 shapes missed them, and the review's wider probe found them. Three reasons that say the work was split went from refused to accepted ("too big and the work was split into parts", "too big and therefore split", "too big and tracked in children 12 and 13"), because a "too" or "not" earlier in the reason stayed in force across an "and" unless the split came right after it. "tests: 5 files, 600" and "test files: 5 (600)" went from refused to accepted too, because the file count was cut out and the second number left behind had no unit. The file-count pattern also took over a second on a line with 65,000 spaces after "tests". #529 closes these three. A few other shapes still move on purpose and fail safe: other negations joined by "and" or "but" to a split, other file counts under 10, and a split phrase followed by a modal that is not a consequence ("splitting into parts would help"). One shape got stricter by the new arrow: a note like "Size → small, 3 lines of copy" is now read as a size, which fails safe with a clear message.
