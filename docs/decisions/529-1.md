# Decision 529-1

- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Decision:** `check-issue-size.mjs` closes the three loosenings the review of #523 found in #433's change (#529). (1) **"so", "and", "then" and "but" end a clause again, unless what stands between the joiner and the split carries the negation on**: one or two words and then "to" ("not so easy to split into parts", "too risky and costly to split into parts", "hard to review but harder to split into parts"), or one manner adverb ("cannot be safely and cleanly split into parts"). So "too big and the work was split into parts", "too big and therefore split" and "too big and tracked in children 12 and 13" are refused again, and every shape #433 names still passes. The rule reads the whole reason, as #433 did. (2) **A file count with another number after it is a second estimate**: "tests: 5 files, 600", "test files: 5 (600)" and "tests: 7 files of 100" are refused. A count with nothing numeric after it ("test files: 2", "tests – 2 new files", "tests: 2 files, 3d viewer") still passes. (3) **The tests-line match is linear**: the lookbehind of the file-count pattern now sits after the digit, so a run of separators is read once. A line with 65,000 spaces after "tests" took about 1.1 s and now takes a few milliseconds. (4) **Decision 433-1's "Why" is corrected** to say which shapes moved.
- **Options considered:** For (1), a longer list of filler words before the split (the review's own idea: open-ended, every new word is a new gap), a negation that reaches only the last few words before the split (can't tell "and the work was split" from "but harder to split", both about the same distance), or the "to" and manner-adverb rule (chosen: a word and "to" is how a negated predicate goes on; a subject, a verb in the past or "therefore" is how a new statement starts). For (2), refuse any number anywhere after a file count (also refuses "tests: 2 files, 3d viewer"), or refuse no file count at all (brings back "test files: 2" and "tests – 2 new files", which #433 frees), or leave a count in place when another plain number follows it, with at most one word between (chosen). For (3), cap the length of a part (hides the slowness, and a long part would pass unread), or move the lookbehind after the digit (chosen).
- **Why:** The check guards against honest mistakes and is a merge gate, so this only tightens: it refuses what #433 let through by accident and accepts nothing new. A few shapes that #433 accepted by accident stay accepted because they are the same class as a named one: other negations joined by "to" ("too big and easy to split into parts"), other file counts under 10, and a split phrase followed by a modal that is not a consequence. The manner adverbs are a short list on purpose; a real reason with another adverb is refused with a clear message, which costs a person one edit.
- **Originator:** AI pick, not reviewed (Full automation). The issue gives the four changes. The picks are: the "to" and manner-adverb rule and its adverb list; one word allowed between a file count and the second number; moving the lookbehind rather than capping a part; and the new test file name.
